## 🎯 The Vision (Why Mediotic?)
In today's fast-paced world, accessing reliable healthcare shouldn't be a struggle. Mediotic was built to eliminate long pharmacy queues and complicated diagnostic bookings. We provide a **unified, seamless, and secure** platform where users can order medicines, upload prescriptions, and book lab tests—all within a few clicks.

## 🏗️ Core System Modules

### 1️⃣ Digital Pharmacy (E-Commerce)
*   **Smart Prescription Upload:** Users can instantly upload handwritten or digital doctor prescriptions to get automated cart fulfillment and up to 15% off.
*   **Dynamic Categorization:** Shop effortlessly across structured categories including *Skin Care, Baby Care, Ayurveda, Vitamins, Diabetes, and Covid Care*.
*   **Trending & Best Offers:** A dedicated algorithm-driven section showcasing high-demand products, daily essentials, and bundled discounts.

### 2️⃣ Diagnostics & Home Services
*   **Home Lab Tests:** Seamless booking flow for *Blood Tests, Sugar Tests, and Full Body Checkups* right from the homepage.
*   **Express Sample Collection:** Guaranteed 2-hour home sample collection with verified phlebotomists.
*   **Expert Consultation:** Direct integration for booking online doctor appointments and telehealth services.

### 3️⃣ Trust & Order Management
*   **Live Order Tracking:** Real-time visual progress bar showing order status (e.g., "Delivery in Progress", "Arriving in 45 mins").
*   **Social Proof & Reviews:** Dedicated testimonial sections showcasing trust from *50,000+ verified patients*.
*   **Guarantees:** Licensed Pharmacy verification, 100% genuine products, and hassle-free returns.
